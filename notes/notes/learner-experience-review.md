# Learner Experience Review Notes

When reviewing a digital learning platform, it is useful to consider how technical and product decisions affect the learner's overall experience.

## Product Review Questions

- Can learners easily understand what they need to do next?
- Are important actions and navigation options easy to find?
- Is feedback clear when a learner completes an action or encounters a problem?
- Do configuration or integration choices create unnecessary friction in the learner journey?

These questions provide a simple product-level checklist for reviewing the learner experience across an Open edX implementation.
